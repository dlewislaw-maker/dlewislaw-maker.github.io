SyncBack Drive Backup

SyncBack Drive Backup is a private, single-user backup configuration. It lets the owner's own installed copy of SyncBackPro (a desktop backup program published by 2BrightSparks) copy files from the owner's computer to the owner's own Google Drive account.

It is not a public service. No other users are invited to sign in, nothing is sold, and no data is collected by this website.
